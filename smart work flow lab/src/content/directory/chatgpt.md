  - question: Is ChatGPT free?
    answer: Yes, ChatGPT has a free tier with access to a capable model and a limited number of messages per day before it switches to a smaller model. Paid plans (Go at $8/mo, Plus at $20/mo, Pro at $200/mo) raise the limits and add features like image, voice, and video.

  - question: Does ChatGPT train on my conversations?

ChatGPT is the AI assistant that started the current wave — and in 2026 it remains the most popular and most flexible option for general use. Built by OpenAI, it handles a huge range of tasks: drafting emails, summarising documents, answering questions, generating and understanding images, holding spoken conversations, and writing or debugging code.

Its biggest strength is breadth. Where some tools specialise, ChatGPT tries to do a bit of everything well, wrapped in an interface that's forgiving for beginners. That makes it the natural first stop for anyone new to AI, and a solid daily driver for most people.

The trade-offs worth knowing: the free and cheaper Go tiers now show ads in the US, and ChatGPT trains on your conversations by default unless you opt out. For sensitive work, that's worth configuring before you start.
