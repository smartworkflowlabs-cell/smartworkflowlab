Claude, built by Anthropic, has earned a reputation as the thinking person's AI assistant. It's particularly strong at the tasks where accuracy and nuance matter most: working through long documents, reasoning carefully rather than rushing to an answer, and writing and debugging code.

For developers, Claude's standout feature is Claude Code — a capable command-line coding agent included on every paid plan, with no separate subscription. Combined with Claude's leading performance on coding benchmarks, that makes it a favourite among technical users.

Claude keeps the feature set focused: you won't find native image or video generation here the way you do in ChatGPT. What you get instead is a careful, capable assistant with privacy-friendly defaults — it doesn't train on your conversations unless you choose to let it.
