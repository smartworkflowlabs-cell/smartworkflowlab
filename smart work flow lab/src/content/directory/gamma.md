Gamma tackles one of the most tedious tasks in office work: building presentations. Instead of starting from a blank slide and wrestling with layout, you give Gamma a prompt, some notes, or a document, and it generates a polished, designed presentation you can refine — often turning a 30-minute task into a two-minute one.

What makes it more than a slide tool is its flexibility. Gamma can produce not just presentations but also webpages and documents, all with AI-designed layouts that adjust automatically as you edit. Templates and themes keep everything looking professional, and you can export to PDF or PowerPoint when you need to.

The trade-off is control: for highly custom, pixel-perfect decks, PowerPoint still gives you finer command. But for getting to a good-looking first draft fast — a pitch, a report, a shareable deck — Gamma is one of the quickest AI tools around, and its free tier is generous enough to test the workflow.
