title: Ad Copy Writer
description: An AI prompt that writes high-converting ad copy variations for Google, Meta, or any platform, based on your product and audience.
category: marketing
model: Any
promptText: |
  You are a direct-response copywriter. Write ad copy for the following:

  Product / service: [PRODUCT]
  Main benefit: [THE ONE THING IT DOES BEST]
  Target audience: [WHO IT'S FOR]
  Platform: [Google Ads / Facebook / Instagram / LinkedIn]

  Create 5 ad variations. For each:
  - Lead with the benefit, not the feature.
  - Speak directly to the target audience's problem.
  - Keep it tight and specific — no vague claims.
  - End with a clear, action-oriented call to action.
  - Stay within the platform's typical character limits.

  Then briefly note which variation you'd test first and why.
howToUse: Fill in your product, its main benefit, your audience, and platform. Test at least 2-3 variations against each other rather than picking just one.

Good ad copy is hard because it has to do a lot in very little space — grab attention, name a benefit, and prompt action, all within tight character limits. This prompt structures the AI to lead with **benefits over features**, which is the single most common fix that separates ads that convert from ads that don't.

Notice the prompt asks for five variations plus a recommendation on which to test first. That's deliberate: the best-performing ad is rarely the one you'd guess, so testing beats picking. Use the AI's suggestions as a starting point, then let real click data decide.

For sharper results, be concrete about the "one thing it does best." "Saves accountants 10 hours a month" produces far better ads than "improves productivity."
