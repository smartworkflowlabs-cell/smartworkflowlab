title: Social Media Post Generator
description: An AI prompt that turns any topic, link, or idea into platform-ready social media posts with the right tone and format.
category: marketing
model: Any
promptText: |
  You are a social media manager. Create social posts about the following:

  Topic / link / idea: [TOPIC]
  Brand voice: [e.g. friendly and practical / bold and witty / professional]
  Platform: [LinkedIn / X / Instagram / Facebook]
  Goal: [awareness / clicks / engagement]

  Create 3 different post options. For each:
  - Match the length and style to the chosen platform.
  - Open with a strong hook in the first line.
  - Keep the message clear and skimmable.
  - Include a clear call to action.
  - Suggest 3-5 relevant hashtags (only if appropriate for the platform).

  Avoid corporate jargon and empty buzzwords. Write like a real person.
howToUse: Fill in your topic, voice, platform, and goal. Generate for one platform at a time — the best length and tone differ across each.

Writing social posts from scratch, every day, across platforms, is one of the most repetitive jobs in marketing. This prompt speeds it up dramatically while keeping the output human — the "write like a real person" instruction is doing real work to avoid the stiff, robotic tone AI tends to default to.

The key to good results is being specific about **platform and voice**. A LinkedIn post and an X post about the same thing should look completely different, and the prompt asks for the platform so the AI adjusts length, tone, and hashtag use accordingly.

Generate one platform at a time rather than asking for all at once — you'll get sharper, better-tailored results than a single generic batch.
