title: Meta Description Writer
description: An AI prompt that writes click-worthy, SEO-optimised meta descriptions under 155 characters for any page or article.
category: seo
model: Any
promptText: |
  You are an SEO copywriter. Write 5 meta description options for the following page.

  Page title: "[TITLE]"
  Target keyword: "[KEYWORD]"
  What the page is about: [BRIEF DESCRIPTION]

  Rules for each meta description:
  - Maximum 155 characters (count carefully).
  - Include the target keyword naturally, ideally near the start.
  - Write in an active voice with a clear benefit or reason to click.
  - No clickbait or false promises.
  - Vary the angle across the 5 options (benefit-led, question-led, curiosity-led, etc.).

  Output as a numbered list, and show the character count in brackets after each one.
howToUse: Fill in the title, keyword, and a short description of the page. Pick the option that best matches your page and click intent.

Meta descriptions don't directly change your ranking, but they heavily influence whether people click your result — and click-through rate does matter. A dull, auto-generated description leaves clicks on the table; a sharp one can lift traffic without changing your position at all.

This prompt gives you five varied options with character counts, so you can pick the angle that best fits your page. The character-count instruction matters: Google truncates descriptions past roughly 155 characters, so anything longer gets cut off with an ellipsis.

Tip: give the AI a real sense of the page's benefit in the brief description. "A free calculator that shows automation savings" produces far better results than "an article about automation."
