Planning content in scattered notes and half-remembered ideas is how good content plans fall apart. This Google Sheets content calendar brings everything into one place: what you're publishing, when, on which platform, and what stage each piece is at.

It's built around the way content actually gets made — ideas become drafts, drafts get scheduled, scheduled posts go live — with a status column that lets you see your whole pipeline at a glance. Dropdown menus for platform, format, and status keep everything consistent, so you can filter and sort your calendar in a click.

To fill your calendar faster, pair it with our [social media post generator prompt](/prompts/marketing/marketing-social-post), and if you want to automate publishing once you've planned, see our [social media automation recipe](/workflows/automate-social-media-posting).
