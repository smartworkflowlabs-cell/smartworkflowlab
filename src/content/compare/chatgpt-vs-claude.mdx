ChatGPT and Claude are the two most popular AI assistants in 2026, and if you're only going to pay for one, the choice matters. They're priced almost identically and both are excellent — but they've been built with different strengths, and the right pick depends on what you actually do all day.

We've spent serious time in both. Here's the honest breakdown.

## The quick verdict

**ChatGPT** is the better all-rounder — it does the widest range of things (writing, images, voice, video, custom GPTs) and is the more forgiving, feature-rich choice for general use. **Claude** is the specialist's pick — stronger at coding, long documents, and careful reasoning, with more privacy-friendly defaults.

## Pricing: essentially a tie

There's almost nothing to separate them on price. ChatGPT Plus and Claude Pro both cost $20 a month, though Claude drops to $17 if you pay annually. Both have genuinely useful free tiers, and both offer higher tiers for heavy users — ChatGPT Pro at $200 a month, and Claude's Max plans at $100 and $200.

One subtle difference: Claude includes Claude Code (its coding agent) on every paid plan at no extra cost, which adds real value for developers. If coding matters to you, Claude quietly offers more for the same $20.

## Coding: Claude's edge

For writing and debugging code, Claude has become the favourite among many developers. It leads most published coding benchmarks and bundles Claude Code, a capable command-line agent, into every paid plan. If your work involves serious coding, that combination is hard to beat.

ChatGPT is no slouch — it has strong coding ability and its own developer tools — but on pure code quality, Claude tends to come out ahead.

## Writing and reasoning: Claude for depth, ChatGPT for range

Both write well, but they shine in different places. Claude is exceptional with long documents and careful, structured reasoning — feed it a lengthy contract or report and it holds the thread better than most. Its large context window helps here.

ChatGPT is the more versatile writer, and crucially it pairs writing with a much broader feature set. If you want to write a blog post _and_ generate an image for it _and_ dictate edits by voice, ChatGPT does all of that in one place.

## Features: ChatGPT's advantage

This is where ChatGPT pulls clearly ahead. It offers native image generation, advanced voice mode, video generation (Sora on the Pro tier), and custom GPTs you can build without code. Claude keeps its feature set more focused — powerful for text, coding, and documents, but without native image or video generation.

If you want an AI that does a bit of everything, ChatGPT's breadth is a real draw.

## Privacy: Claude's defaults are friendlier

For privacy-conscious users, Claude has the edge. Anthropic makes training on your conversations a user choice, and left off, Claude doesn't train on your chats. ChatGPT trains on your conversations by default unless you opt out in the settings. Neither should be trusted with passwords or sensitive secrets, but Claude's defaults require less configuration to keep your data out of training.

## So which should you choose?

**Choose ChatGPT if** you want the most versatile, feature-rich assistant — image and video generation, voice, custom GPTs, and a forgiving all-rounder for everyday tasks. It's the best single AI for most people.

**Choose Claude if** your priorities are coding, working with long documents, careful reasoning, or privacy-friendly defaults. It's the specialist's choice, and developers in particular tend to prefer it.
