Claude and Gemini take very different paths to the same goal. Claude, from Anthropic, is a focused assistant built around careful reasoning and coding. Gemini, from Google, is woven into the tools you probably already use every day. Priced within a dollar of each other, the right choice comes down to where you work and what you need.

## The quick verdict

**Claude** is the pick for coding, careful reasoning, long-document work, and privacy. **Gemini** wins if you live in Google Workspace, need the largest context window, or want AI bundled with cloud storage.

## Pricing: near-identical, different bundles

Claude Pro is $20 a month ($17 billed annually); Google AI Pro is $19.99. The sticker prices are effectively the same, so the real question is what's bundled.

Gemini's plan includes 2TB of Google One cloud storage and deep integration with Gmail, Docs, and Sheets — genuinely good value if you'd pay for storage anyway. Claude includes Claude Code, its coding agent, which is the better bundle for developers. Same price, different value depending on who you are.

## Google integration: Gemini's home advantage

This is Gemini's biggest draw. It's built directly into Gmail, Docs, Sheets, and Android, so it can draft emails, summarise documents, and analyse spreadsheets without you leaving the app you're in. If your work lives in Google Workspace, that convenience is hard to overstate.

Claude is a standalone destination — you go to it, rather than it coming to you. Powerful, but not embedded in your daily tools the way Gemini is.

## Context window: Gemini's clear win

Gemini's Pro model offers a 1M-token context window, the largest among mainstream assistants. For anyone working with very long documents, large codebases, or extensive reference material in one conversation, that headroom is a real, practical advantage.

## Coding and reasoning: Claude's strength

For coding and careful reasoning, Claude leads. It tops most coding benchmarks and includes Claude Code on paid plans, making it the developer favourite. Gemini is capable — particularly on agentic tasks inside Google's ecosystem — but Claude is the stronger choice when code quality and careful reasoning are the priority.

## Privacy: Claude's defaults win

Claude doesn't train on your conversations unless you opt in — a privacy-friendly default that requires no configuration. Gemini handles data through your Google account controls and trains by default. For privacy-conscious users, Claude is the lower-effort choice.

## So which should you choose?

**Choose Gemini if** you live in Gmail, Docs, and Android, want AI built directly into those tools, need the largest context window, or value the bundled 2TB of storage.

**Choose Claude if** your priorities are coding, careful reasoning, long-document work, or privacy — and you don't need deep Google integration.
