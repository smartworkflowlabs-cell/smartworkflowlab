Canva AI brings artificial intelligence into the design platform that made graphic design accessible to everyone. Rather than a standalone image generator, it's a set of AI features layered into Canva's familiar drag-and-drop editor — so you can generate images, edit photos, write copy, and even create whole designs from a text description without leaving your project.

Its biggest strength is accessibility. Where dedicated tools like Midjourney demand prompt skill, Canva AI is built for non-designers: Magic Edit lets you add or remove elements from an image with a click, background removal is instant, and AI can generate a design from a simple description. Combined with Canva's enormous template library, it lets small businesses and creators produce professional-looking visuals fast.

The trade-offs are the usual ones for an all-in-one tool: AI credits are limited on lower tiers, and you get less fine control than dedicated professional software. But for quick, good-looking marketing and social visuals, Canva AI is one of the easiest options available.
