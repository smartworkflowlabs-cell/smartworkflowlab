Gemini is Google's AI assistant, and its biggest advantage isn't a benchmark score — it's location. Where ChatGPT and Claude are separate destinations you visit, Gemini is built directly into Gmail, Docs, Sheets, and Android, sitting inside the tools most people already use every day.

That integration is the whole pitch. If your work lives in Google Workspace, Gemini can draft emails, summarise documents, and analyse spreadsheets without you leaving the app you're in. The paid Google AI Pro plan also bundles 2TB of cloud storage, which makes its $19.99 price genuinely good value if you'd pay for storage anyway.

Gemini also offers the largest context window of the major assistants — a full 1M tokens on the Pro model — making it a strong choice for anyone working with very long documents. The trade-off: if you don't use Google's ecosystem, much of its advantage disappears, and its credit-based usage limits can be harder to predict than competitors' simpler caps.
