Notion AI isn't a standalone chatbot — it's AI woven directly into the Notion workspace millions of people already use for notes, docs, and team knowledge. That's the whole point: instead of copying text into a separate tool, you get AI writing, summarising, and Q&A right where your content lives.

Its strongest features play to that integration. Notion AI can summarise long pages and meeting notes, answer questions across your entire workspace, auto-fill database properties, and rewrite or translate text in place. For teams with a lot of accumulated knowledge in Notion, the workspace-wide Q&A alone can be a real time-saver.

The catch is straightforward: Notion AI is only valuable if you use Notion, and it's an add-on cost on top of your existing plan. For open-ended tasks it's less powerful than a dedicated assistant like ChatGPT or Claude. But for keeping AI inside the workspace you already work in, it's a natural fit.
