Perplexity takes a different approach from the other assistants on this list. Instead of a general-purpose chatbot, it's built as an "answer engine" — you ask a question, it searches the live web, and it gives you an answer backed by citations you can click and verify.

That focus on sourcing is its defining strength. For research, fact-checking, or any question where you need to know _where_ the answer came from, Perplexity is hard to beat. It also routes queries between top models from different providers, so you get the best available model for each task without locking into one ecosystem.

The trade-offs: it's less suited to open-ended creative work than a full chatbot, and its Deep Research feature is now capped at 20 uses per month, making it better for sourced search than heavy report generation. For quick, verifiable answers, though, the free tier alone is genuinely useful.
