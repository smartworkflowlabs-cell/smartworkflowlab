title: Cold Outreach Email Writer
description: An AI prompt that writes short, personalised cold emails that get replies — without sounding like spam.
category: email
model: Any
promptText: |
  You are an expert at writing cold outreach emails that get replies. Write a cold email for the following:

  Who I am: [YOUR ROLE / COMPANY]
  Who I'm emailing: [THEIR ROLE / COMPANY]
  Why I'm reaching out: [THE REASON / OFFER]
  What I want them to do: [THE ASK — e.g. a 15-min call]

  Rules:
  - Keep it under 120 words. Short emails get more replies.
  - Open with something relevant to them, not about me.
  - Make the value clear in one or two sentences.
  - One single, low-friction call to action.
  - Friendly and human — no corporate jargon, no fake urgency.
  - Write a subject line under 6 words that isn't clickbait.

  Output the subject line and the email body.
howToUse: Fill in the four fields. Personalise the first line further with a real detail about the recipient before sending — that lifts reply rates the most.

Cold emails fail for predictable reasons: they're too long, they're all about the sender, and they ask for too much too soon. This prompt fixes all three by capping the length, forcing a recipient-focused opening, and limiting you to one low-friction ask.

The under-120-words rule matters more than people expect. Busy people skim, and a short email that respects their time gets far more replies than a detailed pitch. The prompt bakes that discipline in.

One thing the AI can't do for you: genuine personalisation. Before sending, add one real, specific detail about the recipient to that first line — a recent post, a company update, a shared connection. That single human touch does more for reply rates than any template.
