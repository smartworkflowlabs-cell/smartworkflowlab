---
title: Blog Post Outline Generator
description: An AI prompt that turns a target keyword into a complete, SEO-friendly blog post outline with headings, sections, and search intent.
category: seo
model: Any
promptText: |
  You are an expert SEO content strategist. Create a detailed blog post outline for the target keyword: "[KEYWORD]".

  Requirements:
  - Start with the likely search intent behind this keyword (informational, commercial, or transactional).
  - Suggest an SEO-optimised H1 title (under 60 characters) that includes the keyword naturally.
  - Provide a logical structure of H2 and H3 headings that fully covers the topic.
  - Under each heading, add a one-line note on what to cover.
  - Suggest 3-5 related keywords or questions to include for topical depth.
  - End with a suggested meta description (under 155 characters).

  Keep the outline practical and focused on genuinely helping the reader, not keyword stuffing.
howToUse: Replace [KEYWORD] with your target keyword. For best results, tell the AI your audience and the article's goal after pasting the prompt.

This prompt takes the guesswork out of planning SEO content. Instead of staring at a blank page, you give the AI a keyword and get back a complete, intent-driven outline you can start writing from immediately.

The prompt is deliberately structured to force the AI to think about **search intent first** — the single most important thing to get right in SEO. An outline that matches what searchers actually want will always outperform one that just repeats the keyword.

Customise it by adding your audience and goal. For example, after pasting, add: "The audience is small business owners with no technical background, and the goal is to get them to try our free tool." The more context you give, the sharper the outline.
