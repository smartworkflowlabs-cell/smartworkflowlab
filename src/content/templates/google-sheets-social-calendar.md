Consistency is what makes social media work, and consistency comes from planning ahead. This Google Sheets social media calendar gives you one simple place to plan every post across every platform — so you're never scrambling for something to post.

It's built as a structured spreadsheet, which means it's not just for planning: you can connect it to an automation tool to publish posts automatically. Colour-coding by platform and a status column keep everything clear at a glance.

To turn this calendar into a hands-off posting system, follow our [social media automation recipe](/workflows/automate-social-media-posting), and use the [marketing prompts](/prompts/marketing) to draft your posts faster.
