For freelancers and small teams, a full-blown CRM is often overkill — but tracking leads in your head or a messy spreadsheet doesn't work either. This simple Google Sheets CRM sits in the sweet spot: structured enough to keep every contact and deal organised, simple enough to actually maintain.

It has separate tabs for contacts and deals, plus a pipeline summary that automatically counts your deals and totals their value by stage — so you always know where things stand. It's free, flexible, and takes minutes to set up.
